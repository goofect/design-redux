Controls

Plain. An icon button is its glyph; a text button an underlined word; the one that commits black with white words; a field a line under its text; a page a word. Hover underlines, focus is a 2px outline, on or open is inverted, disabled is grey. Nothing else.

Icon buttonOnly where a glyph is plainer than a word. Its word is its tooltip and its aria-label.
Text button and linkAn underlined word: a link goes somewhere, a text button does something. The one that commits is black, one a screen.
PagesA note's pages: underlined words, the open one inverted. Many scroll a whole tab at a time, an ellipsis at the edge where more are.
ToggleObsidian's own, squared: 40 by 20, an ink knob on the side that is true; on, inverted. The setting's words beside it.
FieldA line under its text. Its name above, grey, only where the placeholder doesn’t say the same.
Filter partPlain words: a filter's part that takes itself off, or a filter that can be on.

Rows

A name in a list, four units high, parted by its height and never by a rule. Open or picked is inverted; nothing else marks it: no number, no mark, no bold.

RowA folder or a note: its name, and what trails it in grey where it says something.
PropertyName and value on a line, the name grey. A group’s title over its rows only where there is more than one group.

The library

Drawn by the plugin itself on a sample vault. Two framed panes, no buttons at rest. The sidebar: the vault’s folders as a tree. The page: a folder’s path as boxed words, then only the notes in it, as ruled rows; its subfolders are in the tree.

The note, and the panel beside it

Round the note’s title, in its own column: the folder’s name as a link over it; under it its properties as one line of plain text (a value its folder’s schema finds wrong in red, with why) and its pages as words, the open one inverted; a line; the text; the strip at its foot, one line. The panel: name and value on a line, the name grey; a group’s title where there are groups; where the note is named, mentioned and what it links to as plain rows. No control on either.

Search, and a filter

The sidebar searching: what is typed, and what it finds by name and inside notes. A filter in the browse view: its name large, its parts as words that take themselves off, what passes as rows.

The map and the 3D graph

A folder as a tree: words on the ground joined by 1px lines, the one picked inverted, a folded branch counted, a folder’s colour, the person’s own, a small square before its word. The 3D graph: ink squares on lines; the picked note and the open one larger, inverted.

A sheet, and the settings page

Property rules: each said as a sentence, its toggle beside it; a new one written as a sentence with fields. The settings page: the features’ switches in two columns, and nothing else.
