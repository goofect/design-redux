Controls

Each state names what carries it besides colour: ash is the pointer, an inner line is the press, the ring is the keyboard, a bar along the foot is on, a dashed line is disabled.

Icon buttonA control. Four units square, the glyph two; its word is its tooltip and its aria-label.
Text buttonOnly where a glyph would not say it: a confirmation, a first choice.
Zoom controlObjects, shelves, words: the same place at three levels of detail.
TabsA note's pages. The label voice; the open page in clay, bold, with the foot bar.
ToggleObsidian's own, restyled. The ink square stands on the side that is true.
FieldA label over a square box. The box carries the border and the ring.
ChipPlain words in a hairline: a property under a note's title, or a filter that can be on.

Objects, rows, bars

The same folder and note at three levels of detail. Clay is never alone: a name appears, a mark stands in the slot, the words turn bold.

Object cellA folder as a glyph on the ground: no frame, no label at rest. Its name is always its aria-label.
RowA note in a list: its number in the slot, its name, what trails. Hairlines between.
ShelfA folder as a row of bars: one a note, as tall as the note is long, standing on a hairline.
Outline rowWords: folders as labels, notes as numbered rows with no rules, the open note’s headings under it.
Panel rowA property: its name in the label voice, its value in the reading voice. A section's title over its rows.

Table, strip, menu, sheet, say-so

The larger pieces, built from the ones above.

TableAn ink grid with one header for the whole of it; the arrow says how it is sorted. Rows: rest, hover, open, selected, a value that is wrong, disabled.
StripAcross the foot of the note: the folder, the note, its place, and on. Nothing floats, nothing waits for the pointer.
MenuObsidian's own, restyled: rows of four units; the item under the pointer or the keys in clay with a line round it.
SheetWhat the plugin puts in one of Obsidian's modals: a title, the matter, the buttons at the foot.
Say-soWhat a surface shows in place of its contents: a glyph, a label, a line if it helps, one button at most.

The library sidebar

One place, three levels of detail, and a folder picked. Each is a sidebar 320px wide and 720 high; what runs past the foot scrolls.

The grid at the sidebar’s widths

Three columns of square cells, each a whole number of units cut from the width: 80px at 280, 104 at 344, 120 at 400. Then labels always shown, and a folder opened: its folders and its notes.

The browse view

The same grid at the window’s width: cells of sixteen units, as many columns as fit, the same zoom control. A folder at the level of words is the table.
